How it works

From a lecture hall to exam-ready in three steps.

1

Record or import

Tap record in class, or bring in audio you already have (MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, AIFF, FLAC).

2

Get the summary

Pick your language and QuickNotes transcribes it, then writes a concise or detailed AI summary.

3

Study smarter

Turn the summary into flashcards and a quiz. Your set is saved, and you can generate a fresh one anytime.

Study mode

Don't just record it. Remember it.

  • Saved with each recording, so it's there when you come back

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is QuickNotes free?

QuickNotes is free to download and use. Some features and higher usage limits are part of optional paid plans, which you can see inside the app.

How do flashcards and quizzes get made?

Once a recording has a summary, QuickNotes builds flashcards and quiz questions from the key points in it. You can generate a new set whenever you finish one.

Which languages are supported?

English, Cantonese, Mandarin (Simplified and Traditional), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic and Hindi. You choose the language when you transcribe.

What audio can I import?

MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, AIFF and FLAC files from your device.

What about my privacy?

We only collect what we need to run your account and improve the app, and we never sell your information. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

Which devices does it run on?

QuickNotes is available for iPhone.
