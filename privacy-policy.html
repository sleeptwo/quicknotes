Privacy Policy

Effective: May 1, 2025

We protect your privacy first. Your notes and recordings are yours alone.

1. What We Never See

  • Your recorded audio content
  • Files imported from your device

2. Basic Information We Use

We only collect:

  • Email address (to create your account)
  • Device type & app version (to fix issues)
  • Feature usage (to improve the app)

All data is aggregated and anonymized where possible.

3. Security Promise

  • Automatic security updates
  • No selling/sharing of your information

4. Your Control

  • Delete all data anytime in settings
  • Export notes with one tap
